Apply

Connect to your CRM

In the CRM, open Profile, then Chrome extension, and make a code.

How it works

  1. In the CRM, open a job and press Apply with the extension.
  2. A tab opens. The helper finds the form, fills each page, and shows a panel.
  3. You read the form and press Submit. It marks the job as applied.

Already on a form? This fills it for the job it matches, or the last job you opened in the CRM.

Writing check

Grammar, plus words that sound machine-made. Runs on this computer only. Shortcut: Alt+Shift+W.
